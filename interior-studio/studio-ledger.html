      How the numbers work
      • Sample data. The studio, its clients and all figures are fictional, built to look like a mid-size Pune practice.
      • Revenue is recognised on percentage of completion: execution value follows cost incurred, design fees spread over the design months.
      • Homes and small shops pay milestones: 10% booking, 15% design sign-off, 35% material advance, 25% mid-execution, 15% handover.
      • Offices and large restaurants pay a 10% mobilisation advance, a monthly RA bill on work done (raised on the 10th for last month), and a 5% retention released 3 months after handover.
      • GST at 18% on all invoices. Input credit on material purchases. Net GST paid on the 20th of the next month.
      • Company clients deduct 2% TDS (section 194C). It comes back as a credit when the studio files its return.
      • Advance tax at 31.2% (LLP, 30% + cess): 15% by 15 Jun, 45% by 15 Sep, 75% by 15 Dec, 100% by 15 Mar.
      • Material suppliers are paid the following month; labour is paid in the month the work is done.
      • Each client pays after its usual delay. "Clients pay later by" adds days to every payment not yet received.
      • A crew delivers about ₹6 L of carpentry, civil and finishing work a month. Work above in-house capacity uses outside crews at 20% more on labour, or waits.
      • The 300 futures vary payment dates (random extra days), remaining costs (±4.5%) and start dates (about 1 in 5 upcoming projects slips a month).
      • Opening bank balance 1 Apr 2026: ₹44 L. Overheads ₹8.5 L a month (salaries, rent, software, travel).
